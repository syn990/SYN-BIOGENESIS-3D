# SYN-BIOGENESIS's features in 3D

What SYN-BIOGENESIS's SYN edition does for Biogenesis 0.9, and where this
program stands on each. Color Mod 2.01 and the Heritage edition are not
simulated here: this program has one simulation and one interface. Color
Mod's beings come in all the same, converted (see the library).

## Done

- **The arena** (ported): the ladder, qualifiers and ladder rounds, sudden
  death, newcomers, the family tree that names species, SETUP, BATTLES,
  TREE and RECORD, the record kept for good, PRINT WINNERS (the ladder's
  `.bgg` files and `winners.txt`; not yet its picture), `--arena` without a
  window and `--arena --enter FILE`. New here: six maps (the default box,
  medium, large, a pond, a cube, the parameters' own) and WATCH, a round
  made again in the window from the record.
- **The commands**: the command list (`/`) with SYN-BIOGENESIS's headings,
  keys and console words; 0.9's actions (new world, open, save as, CO2 up
  and down, kill all, disperse all, a random organism, paste, import; feed,
  weaken, force reproduction, rejuvenate, kill, copy, export, save its
  image, revive, disperse); the card and right-click menus as
  SYN-BIOGENESIS's; its keys; the main menu, OPTIONS (themes, fullscreen)
  and the file browser.
- **The laboratory**: SYN-BIOGENESIS's Genetic Laboratory as a screen
  (genes typed or stepped, inserted, deleted; colours with the field
  guide's lines; symmetry, mirror, dispersed children; Copy to clipboard,
  Cancel, Clear, Import, Keep; the code kept until copied or cancelled),
  opened from the bar, `G`, the card's Genes, the organism's menu and any
  being shown (a click; Open in the laboratory on a right click). New
  here: each gene's bend, the drives (or those its body asks for), and the
  body turning in 3D, dragged round and tilted.
- **The library** as SYN-BIOGENESIS's: folders made, renamed and removed;
  beings renamed, moved, deleted to the Bin and emptied from it, put on
  the ladder, into the world, into the laboratory, copied; IMPORT; Keep
  from everywhere a being is shown; the arena's ladder as a live folder;
  the field guide under the folders. New here: SYN-BIOGENESIS's own
  libraries read beside it, 0.9's and Color Mod's, and a new world started
  with one. Any `.bgg` is read the same converged way: Color Mod's colours
  become the nearest of this program's by what they do (its methane
  eater, dark gray, becomes purple), branched genes are straightened, and
  what an older being lacks (the height its line keeps to, its drives, its
  body plan) is worked out from its body rather than refused; the card
  says what was converted.
- **The telescope and Where it lives**, worked out again for this
  program's genesis: the dice's algebra as it was (the generator is 0.9's,
  and lengths and turns are drawn as 0.9 drew them); a founder's run of
  draws re-derived (gene j's length 5 + 6j + 2m draws in, m the bent genes
  before it); a world's seed read back from its save and proved by replay;
  a being's ancestor, its seeds and its later draws, with bends and drives
  placed as well as lengths and turns; the arena's rounds searched; scenes
  opened at their frame; FORGE A WINNER, its seeds weighed by a fit made
  again on 300 3D worlds (`docs/crack`). `--telescope jump|seed|gene|
  world|find|crack|forge|basins|scene`. Not ported: `fade`, a research
  tool of SYN-BIOGENESIS's with no screen.
- **Create a winner**: SYN-BIOGENESIS's search, on every core, with places
  in the water; the winner pasted where it was worked out, and the
  window's verdict when the calculated time comes (checked: an independent
  replay matched the predicted hash, and the window said "as
  calculated"). `--winner WORLD.b3w`.
- **Looks**: Classic, Biological, Galaxy and Digital, SYN-BIOGENESIS's
  palettes, each with a scene of its own in 3D (the water's colour and
  fog; Biological's sediment floor, caustics, shadows, light shafts,
  surface and motes; a starfield and stronger bloom; grids on every wall),
  `L` and the command list, kept with the theme. New here: bodies drawn as
  flesh, each colour a tissue, on screen and in every being shown, with
  less detail far off; Classic as plain lines, the fastest.
- **The view**: the minimap (above and from the side here), Actual size
  (`0`), Fit the world, zoom in and out, tracking; the looks and the
  minimap in OPTIONS; the user manual and About in the command list.
- **Statistics** as SYN-BIOGENESIS has them: OVERVIEW, CHARTS (0.9's
  twelve, the water's three, and methane as SYN-BIOGENESIS shows Color
  Mod's, beside the other gases in OVERVIEW and on the bar too), EPOCHS, the FAMILY tree (live, named
  species, export) and TIMELINE, kept beside a saved world; 0.9's
  WorldStatistics kept by the world; the card's species and Family.
- **Parameters**, kept between runs (`parameters` in the configuration
  folder) and typed.
- **The console**, the bar, notifications, the right-click menu, the
  organism card and the key hints (the shell).
- **Saved worlds**, the field guide and kinds of being.
- **Scripted input** for tests (`SYN3D_SCRIPT`, as `SYN_BIOGENESIS_SCRIPT`).

## Left out

- Color Mod 2.01 and the Heritage edition as simulations: other rules
  and an older interface. Color Mod's beings import converted; its
  methane is here as this program's own (purple, `docs/DESIGN.md`).
- The telescope's `fade`, a research tool with no screen.
